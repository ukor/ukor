# Hi, I'm Jidechi

I am a senior software engineer with a mad obsession for distributed systems and high-frequency infrastructure. At the core of it all, I am fascinated by how we taught rocks to communicate in ones and zeros, and how those invisible bits power massive scale. 

I focus on building resilient backend systems, real-time data pipelines, and clean architectures where predictability and stability are paramount.

* **First Principles:** I believe in learning language internals from scratch and deeply understanding the tools I use, preferring hands-on mastery over automated code generation.

* **Bare Metal to High Level:** From configuring native Linux process management to training transformer models, I love operating across the entire stack.
